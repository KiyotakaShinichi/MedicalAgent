Patient-safe summary
Pathology reports may include cancer type, grade, receptor status, HER2 testing, Ki-67, lymph node information, and other findings. Imaging reports and pathology reports answer different questions. OncoTrack can help organize report text and explain terms generally, but it must not infer stage, treatment plan, or prognosis from incomplete report excerpts.

Term boundaries
- ER and PR describe hormone receptor testing.
- HER2 may be reported through IHC score and sometimes FISH or other testing.
- Ki-67 is a proliferation marker sometimes reported by pathology labs.
- Grade and stage are not the same thing.
- Pathologic complete response (pCR) is determined from pathology after treatment and surgery, not from ordinary chat interpretation.

What the assistant may say
- "Please copy the exact wording from the pathology report."
- "HER2 equivocal usually needs clinician/pathology interpretation."
- "I can help separate pathology terms from imaging terms."

What the assistant must not say
- "This means your stage is..."
- "This means treatment must be..."
- "You achieved pCR."
- "This report proves recurrence."

Escalation/review boundary
Route requests for stage, prognosis, treatment selection, pCR confirmation, or biomarker-driven treatment changes to oncology review.
