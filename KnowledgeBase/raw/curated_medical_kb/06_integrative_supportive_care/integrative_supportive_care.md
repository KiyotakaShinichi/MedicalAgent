# Integrative and Supportive Care During Breast Cancer Treatment

## Purpose

This document supports safe education about supportive care. It distinguishes symptom support from cancer treatment. Integrative approaches should be discussed with the oncology team, especially during active treatment.

## Exercise and physical activity

Appropriate physical activity may support fatigue, function, and quality of life for some patients. Activity should be adapted to symptoms, blood counts, surgery restrictions, neuropathy, balance, and clinician guidance.

## Nutrition basics

Nutrition during treatment should focus on safe, adequate intake, hydration, and managing symptoms such as nausea, mouth sores, appetite changes, diarrhea, or constipation. The assistant should not prescribe a diet as cancer treatment.

## Sleep and stress management

Sleep routines, relaxation, breathing exercises, meditation, and psychosocial support may help some patients cope with stress and fatigue. Severe anxiety, depression, hopelessness, or self-harm language needs escalation to human support and emergency resources where appropriate.

## Meditation and yoga

Integrative oncology guidelines have discussed meditation and yoga as supportive approaches for anxiety, stress, mood, and quality of life in some cancer care settings. Patients should adapt practices to physical limitations and clinician advice.

## Acupuncture and acupressure

Guidelines have discussed acupuncture or acupressure for selected symptoms such as chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting or pain in some contexts. Patients should ask the care team first, especially with low platelets, infection risk, lymphedema risk, anticoagulants, or implanted devices.

## Massage and physical therapy

Massage and physical therapy may support comfort or function for some patients, but precautions matter after surgery, radiation, lymphedema, bone lesions, low platelets, infection, or wounds. The assistant should encourage care-team guidance.

## What the assistant must not say

- "This integrative therapy treats cancer."
- "Use this instead of chemotherapy."
- "This is safe for you personally."
- "You do not need to ask your oncology team."
