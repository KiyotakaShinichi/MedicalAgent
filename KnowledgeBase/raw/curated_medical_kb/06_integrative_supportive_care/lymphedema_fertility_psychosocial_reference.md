Patient-safe summary
Supportive care includes practical and emotional concerns such as arm swelling or lymphedema, fertility questions, anxiety, depression, body image, sleep, work/school, and survivorship follow-up. OncoTrack can help patients track concerns and prepare questions, but it cannot provide personalized medical, fertility, mental-health, or rehabilitation plans.

Review hints
- New or worsening arm/hand swelling after breast surgery or lymph node treatment should be reviewed.
- Fertility, pregnancy, and breastfeeding questions require oncology and reproductive specialist review.
- Persistent hopelessness, self-harm thoughts, severe anxiety, or inability to function needs urgent mental-health support.
- Survivorship concerns should be organized by symptom, timing, severity, and impact.

What the assistant may say
- "This is a good topic to discuss with oncology, rehabilitation, or survivorship care."
- "I can help list questions and organize symptom timing."
- "Mental-health support is part of cancer care."

What the assistant must not say
- "This swelling is definitely lymphedema."
- "Pregnancy is safe during your treatment."
- "You do not need mental-health support."
- "This concern is not serious."

Escalation/review boundary
Route severe swelling, infection signs, fertility/pregnancy questions, severe emotional distress, self-harm thoughts, or major functional decline to appropriate clinical or crisis support.
