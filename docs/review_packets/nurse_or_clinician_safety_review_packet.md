# Nurse Or Clinician Safety Review Packet

Status: unreviewed and prepared for future review.

This packet is for future lightweight safety/wording review by an oncology nurse, oncology clinician, or similarly qualified reviewer. It is not a request for clinical validation and should not be interpreted as approval for patient care.

## Review Focus

- Urgent symptom escalation wording.
- Treatment-change refusals.
- Fever/low CBC safety routing.
- Patient-safe language and over-reassurance risk.
- Whether “review with care team” language is clear enough.
- Any wording that could be mistaken for diagnosis, prognosis, or treatment advice.

## Log Template

Use `Data/evals/review_templates/clinical_safety_review_log_template.csv`.

Required fields: reviewer role, date, case ID, output reviewed, comment, severity, required fix, fix status, linked artifact or commit, residual issue.
