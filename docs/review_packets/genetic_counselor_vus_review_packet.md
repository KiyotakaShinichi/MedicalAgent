# Genetic Counselor VUS Review Packet

Status: unreviewed and prepared for future review.

This packet is for future review of genetic counseling readiness, germline/somatic language, and VUS boundaries. It is not genetic counseling, clinical validation, or approval.

## Review Focus

- VUS is not treated as positive or negative.
- Germline and somatic results are not conflated.
- Family-risk wording avoids prediction.
- Patient-specific genetic-risk interpretation routes to genetics-trained review.
- Biomarker/genetic records are framed as organization and education only.
