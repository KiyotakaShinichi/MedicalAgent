# 10/10 under constraints

> **"10/10 under constraints" does NOT mean clinically validated,
> production healthcare ready, hospital deployable, or proven patient
> benefit.** It means as far as this project can credibly go with no
> real patient data, no clinician sign-off, no IRB, and no
> institutional partner.

The corresponding machine-readable artifact lives at
[`Data/evals/governance/latest_10_out_of_10_constraint_roadmap.json`](../Data/evals/governance/latest_10_out_of_10_constraint_roadmap.json)
and is regenerated by
[`scripts/run_ten_out_of_ten_roadmap.py`](../scripts/run_ten_out_of_ten_roadmap.py).
Schema is locked by
[`tests/test_ten_out_of_ten_roadmap.py`](../tests/test_ten_out_of_ten_roadmap.py).

## Why this exists

Every individual eval artifact in this repo answers a narrow
engineering question. None of them, alone, gives a reader an honest
*overall* picture of where the project stands relative to its hard
constraints. This document and its artifact do that — but only under
the explicit framing that **the constraints themselves are a hard
ceiling**.

The single most important number in the artifact is
`real_clinical_readiness`. It is capped at 2.0 / 10 by the test
suite. Any future change that pushes it above 2.0 without real
patient data, real clinician sign-off, and IRB approval is a test
failure, by design.

## The 17 dimensions

The artifact's `dimensions` array carries one entry per dimension
with seven fields:

- `current_score_out_of_10` — conservative; numbers above 8 must be
  defensible from artifacts already in the repo.
- `why_not_higher`
- `strongest_evidence`
- `weakest_evidence`
- `credibility_risk` — how this dimension is most easily overclaimed.
- `what_would_make_it_10_under_constraints` — the finish line.
- `what_cannot_be_solved_without_external_or_real_data_or_irb` — the
  permanent floor inside the constraint set.

## The 15-item ranked roadmap

Items are grouped into four tiers:

- **A. Implement now** — controllable today, no external dependency.
  Five items.
- **B. Needs external reviewer** — controllable but blocks on a
  human reviewer engaging under the no-read protocol. Five items.
- **C. Needs real data** — blocks on access to real cohort data
  under a DUA / restricted-access packet. Three items.
- **D. Needs IRB / institution** — blocks on regulatory /
  institutional engagement out of repo scope. Two items.

The brief's anti-overclaim rule lives in
`REQUIRED_ANTI_OVERCLAIM_TOKENS`; the artifact's `claim_boundary`
field must contain every token, and the test suite enforces this.

## Anti-overclaim invariants (test-locked)

The roadmap doc and JSON artifact must satisfy:

- `clinical_validation` field is `false`.
- `real_clinical_readiness.current_score_out_of_10 <= 2.0`.
- `claim_boundary` contains the phrases `not clinical validation`,
  `not production healthcare ready`, `no clinician sign-off`,
  `no irb`, `no real patient data`.
- Every dimension has all seven required fields.
- Every roadmap item belongs to one of the four tier strings exactly.
- Tiers C / D items have `controllable_now == false`.

If any of these break, the test suite fails and the artifact is
not allowed to ship.

## Things that CANNOT be claimed under the current constraints

Verbatim from the artifact's `things_we_cannot_claim_under_constraints`:

- clinical validation
- real-world safety guarantee
- patient benefit
- diagnostic, treatment, or prognostic authority
- clinician sign-off
- IRB / ethics approval
- regulatory clearance
- production healthcare readiness
- generalisation from synthetic to real patients

## Exact next best human action

The highest-leverage move under the current constraints is the one
that exists outside this repo: get one volunteer reviewer hour. The
review-packet INDEX
([`docs/review_packets/INDEX.md`](review_packets/INDEX.md)) is
designed so that hour is cheap to receive; the held-out v2 RAG
protocol and the source-filter-drop adjudication packet are designed
so the reviewer leaves a structured artifact behind.

If no reviewer is available, the next best controllable move is to
fill the A-tier items in this roadmap (items 1, 2, 3, 9, 10) and
re-rate.
