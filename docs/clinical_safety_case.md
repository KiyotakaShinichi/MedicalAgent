# Clinical Safety Case

## System Purpose
Non-diagnostic breast cancer monitoring engineering prototype.

## Intended Use
Organize patient journey records, educational RAG answers, monitoring signals, and clinician-review workflows.

## Not Intended Use
Diagnosis, treatment recommendation, prognosis, genetic counseling, tumor-marker interpretation, or real patient prediction.

## Hazards
Overtrust, unsafe treatment changes, false reassurance, genetic/VUS overclaim, tumor-marker overclaim, hallucinated citations, privacy leakage.

## Mitigations
Safety gates, source governance, claim validation, post-generation validation, abstention, traceability, release gates, human review.

## Residual Risks
No clinician sign-off, no real data validation, synthetic-only metrics, limited multilingual coverage.

## Future Review Plan
Use advisor packet, review log, and safety cases for future oncology nurse/clinician review.
