# Buyer FAQ

## Is NLCare clinically validated or a medical device?

No. It has no prospective clinical validation, clinician sign-off, real-patient
evidence, IRB/ethics approval, regulatory clearance, or medical-device status.

## Does it contain real patient data?

The core project and buyer demo are synthetic. Some tracked research bridge files
derive from public/restricted datasets under source-specific terms; they are
excluded from the deterministic buyer archive and require independent review.

## Is it production-ready?

It is strong for local technical diligence and a synthetic research demo. Hosted
research SaaS still needs buyer identity, secrets, TLS, managed state, centralized
telemetry, privacy lifecycle, backup/restore operations, security review, and
support ownership. Clinical production is blocked much further upstream.

## Can providers be changed or can it run offline?

Provider seams exist for LLMs, vectors, automation, identity, and ML tracking.
Core verification and the synthetic demo work without cloud keys after declared
dependencies/models/derived artifacts are provisioned. Provider substitution
must preserve safety, evidence, latency, cost, and failure contracts.

## Can the safety framework be reused outside oncology?

Generic routing, evidence, fault, authorization, and evaluation patterns can be
adapted. Medical policies, ontology, prompts, knowledge sources, thresholds, and
evaluation banks are domain-specific and require redesign and expert review.

## Why preserve DEP-001 failures?

They are genuine independent negative evidence. Editing, suppressing, rerunning a
consumed bank, or lowering a threshold would damage scientific integrity. Only a
fresh independently authored no-read evaluation can replace that evidence.

## What must the buyer provision?

Identity/OIDC, cloud and hosting, database/cache, DNS/TLS, secret management,
monitoring/error reporting, LLM/vector/n8n/email accounts if used, model weights,
optional research papers, and all controlled/public dataset access.

## Does source acquisition include third-party assets?

No automatic ownership transfer occurs. Packages, models, data, papers, icons,
and provider services retain their terms. Review the machine license inventory.

## What are the license options?

A non-exclusive source license and a full first-party project/IP acquisition are
technically possible structures. Neither is drafted here; both require ownership,
scope, exclusions, warranties, generated assets, and third-party terms to be
handled professionally.
