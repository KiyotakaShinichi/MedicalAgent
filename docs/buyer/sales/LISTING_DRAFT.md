# NLCare — Healthcare AI Safety, RAG & Evaluation Platform

## One-line summary

A transferable FastAPI + React/TypeScript healthcare-AI research platform for
safety-governed RAG, bounded agents, adversarial evaluation, synthetic temporal
ML/MLE, evidence provenance, and operational observability.

## What is built

- Source-governed sparse/dense RAG with RRF, evidence policies, citations, claim
  checks, trace replay, and negative-result evaluation
- Bounded patient-support agent with deterministic pre/post safety controls,
  structured update workflows, refusal/escalation, and multilingual testing
- Synthetic longitudinal breast-monitoring workflows and ML/MLE/XAI lifecycle
  evidence with abstention, calibration, leakage/shortcut checks, and promotion
  boundaries
- FastAPI backend, React/TypeScript portals, Alembic migrations, Docker profiles,
  CI, offline verification, structured logs, health/readiness, and 2,600+ backend
  plus 300+ frontend tests at the R4 baseline
- Buyer data room, machine manifests, deterministic synthetic demo, archive
  builder, protected-evidence hashes, and canonical verification command

## Buyer receives

The agreed Git source/history, application code, schemas/migrations, tests, CI,
containers, synthetic demo assets, eligible artifacts, and technical handoff
documentation. Third-party rights and buyer-owned external accounts are excluded
or separately reviewed as documented.

## Current status

Pre-commercial research/engineering asset. Represented revenue: **zero**.
Represented customers/users: **zero**. No clinical validation, clinician
approval, real-patient deployment, regulatory clearance, or patient-benefit
claim. DEP-001 held-out behavioral failures remain visible and release-blocking.

## Possible buyer profiles

- Healthcare-AI or medical-NLP teams: domain safety/evidence framework
- AI-evaluation or RAG-reliability teams: adversarial and provenance platform
- AI consultancies: research accelerator and demonstrator
- Synthetic-data teams: longitudinal simulator/evaluation surface
- Universities/labs: reproducible research and governance scaffold
- Internal healthcare-AI teams: nonclinical evaluation tooling foundation

These are possible fits, not evidence of buyer demand.

## Reason for transfer and handoff

The project is being prepared as a transferable technical asset so another team
can continue engineering without undocumented owner-machine knowledge. A
synthetic demo and technical handoff are available. Asking price, support,
warranties, and legal transfer terms are intentionally not defined here.
