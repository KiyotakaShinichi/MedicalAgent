# Executive summary

## What NLCare is

NLCare is a substantial FastAPI and React/TypeScript research platform showing
how a healthcare-AI system can combine source-governed retrieval, bounded agent
actions, deterministic safety layers, claim/citation checks, synthetic temporal
ML, adversarial evaluation, evidence lineage, CI, Docker, and runtime
observability. Its strongest asset is the engineering and evaluation framework,
not a claim that its medical outputs are ready for care.

## What a buyer receives

- Git source/history, backend, frontend, migrations, tests, CI, containers, and
  technical documentation.
- Deterministic synthetic demo data and resettable buyer-demo tooling.
- RAG, agent, safety, ML/MLE, XAI, automation, and evidence-governance code.
- Existing positive and negative evaluation artifacts, protected against silent
  rewriting by a 757-file integrity manifest.
- A configuration matrix, asset/license inventory, operations runbook, package
  builder, and canonical verification command.

The transfer does **not** include personal/cloud accounts, API keys, OAuth
secrets, email accounts, DNS/TLS, hosted services, real patient data, research
paper rights, controlled datasets, clinician approval, regulatory clearance, or
clinical validation.

## Present maturity

Local reproducibility, CI, the synthetic demo, migration mechanics, health
contracts, structured logs, and evidence visibility are strong. Hosted research
SaaS remains partial: buyer-owned identity, secrets, deployment, durable metrics,
error-reporting integration, backup cadence, privacy lifecycle, tenancy review,
and support operations are still required. Clinical production is blocked by
real-data, clinical, regulatory, privacy, security, and DEP-001 evidence gaps.

## Commercial status

NLCare has no represented customers, users, revenue, MRR/ARR, clinical partners,
production deployment, or validated patient benefit. It is a pre-commercial
technical asset. The repository has no root license; first-party ownership and
transfer terms require professional review. External datasets, publications,
models, packages, and provider services retain their own terms.

## Plausible development directions

The architecture could support a healthcare-AI evaluation platform, RAG
reliability tool, adversarial testing service, synthetic benchmark platform,
internal AI-governance system, consulting accelerator, or cross-specialty
research framework. These are possible directions, not demand or outcome claims.
